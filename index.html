About This Book

What This Book Will — and Will Not — Do

This book will not teach you to operate a piece of software. There are manuals for that. What this book will do is show you why the tools fail without a governing discipline — why an organisation can own the most capable modelling software and still produce Level 1 outcomes, because the software was never the constraint. The methodology was.

You will meet that methodology here as the ELEVORE Digital Maturity Framework (EDMF) — a map, built from documented Indian and international evidence, of exactly where an organisation stands today and exactly what closing each gap requires. Every figure is sourced. Every claim is checked. Where evidence is uncertain, the book says so.

"However you arrive, the destination is the same. By the last page of this volume, you will know precisely where your organisation stands on the path to using BIM properly — and precisely what the next step costs, and what it protects."

Who This Book Is For

Every Reader Finds Their Own Path

🎓 Students & New to BIM

Life-transforming for Civil, Mechanical & Electrical branch students (Engineering & Diploma) and working professionals building a strong BIM career. Ch 1 sets the industry crisis; Ch 3 & 5 build your foundation.

💼 Professionals & Architects

Focus on technical middle chapters. Ch 5 maps competency by role. Ch 6 draws the line between CAD and true BIM. Ch 7 explains federated coordination and the CDE.

🏗️ Site Engineers & PMs

Operational chapters speak to daily practice. Ch 8 covers quantity take-off. Ch 11 sets out the P1–P5 clash system. Ch 12 covers mobile field tools and digital QA.

🏛️ CEOs, Directors & Developers

Start wherever "capital" appears in a chapter title. Ch 1 quantifies fragmentation costs nationally. Ch 10 connects model maturity to payment governance.

A Commitment Beyond This Book

The ELEVORE Mission

This book is the opening chapter of a longer undertaking. The mission behind the ELEVORE Digital Construction Series is to help move 50–60% of India's AEC industry to world-class digital governance standards — organisations operating at Level 5 of the EDMF — by 2046.

Capital protection approaching 98% under the CPDGR (Capital Protection through Digital Governance Ratio). Readers of this book will be kept part of that progress — research findings will be published periodically as articles across professional and business platforms.

"The industry has known about this problem for decades. What it has lacked is a structured, honest, India-specific account of what to do about it. That is what follows."
